How It Works

Vault Mechanics

ERC-4626 standardizes yield-bearing vaults. One interface for deposits, withdrawals, and yield — across every creator coin.

01

Deposit

Transfer a creator coin into the vault. The contract mints vault shares proportional to your deposit at the current exchange rate.

02

Earn

Fees accrue to the vault over time. As the vault earns, the value of each share increases — your claim grows without any action.

03

Redeem

Burn your vault shares to withdraw your proportional claim. Deposited asset plus accrued fees — redeemable anytime.
