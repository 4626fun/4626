Problem

The old model is speculation.

Creator coins today are pure price exposure — buy low, sell high, hope you're not last. No claim on the economy, no reward for holding, no fair launch. It's zero-sum: someone always exits holding the bag.

4626 flips the model: hold → earn, not hold → hope 4626.fun
Insight

The rails already exist.

ERC-4626 makes deposits and proportional rewards simple. Creators still capture the majority of what they create — and fans get a real, programmatic share.

We're done with pump-and-dump as the only story. It's time creators and fans earn together.
