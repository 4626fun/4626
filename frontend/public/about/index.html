About

The credible home for creator vaults on Base.

4626.fun is building ERC-4626 creator-vault infrastructure for onchain creator economies. The product thesis is simple: creator coins should be able to live inside redeemable, inspectable vaults instead of only trading as standalone speculative assets.

What 4626.fun is

Standard

ERC-4626 vault layer

4626.fun centers on the ERC-4626 tokenized-vault standard: deposit an underlying asset, receive vault shares, and keep the accounting legible.

Creator asset

Creator coin in, vault share out

The infrastructure is designed around creator coins and share tokens, with explicit risk disclosure and strategy routing.

Launch model

Pre-launch, not live-yet hype

Public vault examples are demo previews. Live claims should only appear when they point to official contracts and docs.
