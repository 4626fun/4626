How it works

Three steps. One standard. Shared yield.

  1. 01

    Creator deploys

    The creator deploys an ERC-4626 vault on Base around their creator coin. The genesis deposit seeds the strategy split.

  2. 02

    Community deposits

    Fans send the creator coin (or another supported asset) to the vault and receive an ERC-4626 share token in return — a transferable receipt that participates in the strategy.

  3. 03

    Strategy settles on-chain

    The vault routes assets across Ajna lend markets, Charm concentrated LP, and a Solana cross-chain leg. Outcomes are variable. The chain is the audit log.

FAQ

Common questions, honestly answered.

What is a creator vault?

An ERC-4626 strategy vault built around a creator coin. Fans deposit the underlying creator coin and receive ERC-4626 vault share tokens that represent a pro-rata claim on the vault's assets and any strategy outcomes. More →

Is 4626.fun audited?

Pre-launch. The contracts are in audit. There are no live deposits. Audit reports and contract addresses will be published at /security as the protocol approaches mainnet.

Where is 4626.fun deployed?

Base (Ethereum L2). Cross-chain strategy legs use LayerZero OFT and bridge into ecosystems like Solana via Meteora. More on the standard →

How do I deposit into a vault?

You can't yet. 4626.fun is in pre-launch. Join the waitlist to get notified when vaults open.

What are the risks?

Vault shares are not bank deposits and are not insured. Creator coins may be volatile or illiquid. Smart contract bugs, strategy losses, bridge failures, zero-fee periods, and delayed redemptions are all possible. Full disclosure →
