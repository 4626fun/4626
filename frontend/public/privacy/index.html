Draft privacy notice

The waitlist should not become a data swamp.

This privacy notice is a minimum public draft for review. It explains the website and waitlist data 4626.fun may collect to operate the product, prevent abuse, and understand launch readiness.

Email Role Optional X handle UTM attribution Hashed IP when needed

What may be collected

Waitlist

Signup fields

4626.fun may collect waitlist email, selected role, optional X handle, referral information, and form submission status.

Attribution

Campaign context

4626.fun may store first-touch and session attribution such as `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`, referrer, visitor ID, session ID, path, and event properties.

How data is used

Operate the waitlistTrack signups, role mix, referrals, launch interest, and approval or outreach status.
Prevent abuseUse rate limits, spam checks, and privacy-preserving attribution signals. Raw IP storage should require a specific legal and operational reason.
Measure conversionUnderstand which pages, links, and campaigns produce useful signups before any paid traffic is considered.
Support usersRespond to contact, deletion, or correction requests tied to the email or account record.

Processors and requests

Processors

4626.fun may use infrastructure and service providers such as Supabase, Vercel, Privy, analytics tools, email tools, and anti-spam providers. This list should be updated as processors are added or removed.

Retention

Retention periods are pending final policy. 4626.fun should keep data only as long as needed for operational, legal, security, and product purposes.

Deletion requests

To request deletion or correction, email hello@4626.fun from the email address associated with your waitlist or account record.

Sensitive data

Do not send seed phrases, private keys, or sensitive credentials to 4626.fun. 4626.fun will never DM you first asking for seed phrases, private keys, or deposits.

Questions or deletion requests: hello@4626.fun
