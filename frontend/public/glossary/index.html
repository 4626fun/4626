Glossary

The vocabulary behind 4626.fun.

Plain-English definitions for the standards, mechanics, and terminology used across the protocol. Each term links to its full explanation.

Terms

Creator vault

An ERC-4626 strategy vault built around a creator coin. Creators deploy. Fans deposit. Outcomes settle on-chain.

ERC-4626

The Ethereum tokenized-vault standard. Defines a uniform interface for any vault that wraps a single ERC-20 underlying asset.

EIP-7540

The asynchronous extension of ERC-4626. Adds a request → claimable → claimed lifecycle for cross-chain or concentrated-LP redemptions.

Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA)

The Uniswap V4-based mechanism used to distribute vault share tokens at launch. Gradual price discovery over a 7-day window.
