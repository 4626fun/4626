Glossary

Creator Vault

An ERC-4626 strategy vault built around a creator coin. Fans deposit the underlying creator coin (or another supported asset) and receive an ERC-4626 share token in return — a transferable receipt for their pro-rata claim on vault assets and strategy outcomes.

How it works

DeployerThe creator. They deploy the vault on Base around their creator coin and configure the strategy split (lending, concentrated LP, cross-chain, idle buffer).
Underlying assetThe creator coin — an ERC-20 launched on Zora, Coinbase Creator Coins, Clanker, or any compatible launcher.
Share tokenAn ERC-4626 share that represents a pro-rata claim on totalAssets(). Transferable, composable, and redeemable subject to vault liquidity.
StrategyConfigurable on deploy. 4626.fun ships allocation primitives for Ajna lend markets, Charm concentrated LP, Solana cross-chain via Meteora, and an idle buffer.
Fee modelHigh-water-mark performance fee in basis points, paid only above the previous share-price peak. Aligned by code.
Withdrawal lifecycleEIP-7540 async by default. Liquid positions redeem in one tx; cross-chain or concentrated-LP positions follow the request → claimable → claimed lifecycle.
