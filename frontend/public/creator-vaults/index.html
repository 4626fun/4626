Creator Vaults

An ERC-4626 vault, built around your creator coin.

A creator vault routes a creator coin into onchain strategies and gives depositors a transferable share token in return. Creators deploy. Fans deposit. The chain settles the math. Pre-launch — vaults are not live yet.

Demo data on 4626.fun is illustrative. Strategy outcomes are variable and may be zero or negative. Read the risks.

How a creator vault works

01

Creator deploys

The creator deploys a vault on Base around their creator coin. The genesis deposit seeds the vault and initializes the strategy split.

02

Community deposits

Depositors send the creator coin (or another supported asset) to the vault and receive an ERC-4626 share token in return — a transferable receipt for their pro-rata claim.

03

Strategy settles on-chain

The vault routes assets across Ajna lend markets, Charm concentrated LP, and a Solana cross-chain leg. Outcomes are variable. The chain is the audit log.

Who creator vaults are for

Creators with a coin

If your creator coin already has a community on Zora, Coinbase Creator Coins, Clanker, or any ERC-20 launcher, a vault gives that coin somewhere to go after the mint — strategy infrastructure that does not require custom contracts.

Fans and depositors

Holding a creator coin in a wallet earns nothing. A vault deposit gives you a share token that participates in onchain strategy outcomes — variable, redeemable subject to liquidity, and transferable across DeFi.

Builders and strategists

Each vault is a configurable allocation across DeFi primitives. Strategists can fork existing vault patterns or propose new ones, and ERC-4626 share tokens compose with everything that already speaks the standard.

Base ecosystem partners

Creator vaults inherit Base's account abstraction surface — Coinbase Smart Wallet compatible, gas-light, Privy-friendly. Onchain Summer aligned.

Aligned by code

High-water-mark performance feeCreators only earn during periods when vault share value rises above its previous peak. Drawdown periods earn no creator fee. Aligned by code, not by promise.
Pro-rata accountingEvery depositor's claim is a function of shares held divided by total shares. Strategy gains and losses settle proportionally. No special tranches.
EIP-7540 async redemptionsLiquid strategy positions support immediate redemption. Cross-chain or concentrated-LP positions settle through the EIP-7540 pending → claimable → claimed lifecycle. What is EIP-7540?
The chain is the audit logEvery deposit, allocation, accrual, fee accrual, and redemption is on-chain. No off-chain "trust me." No hidden vault accounting.

Pre-launch status

Audit in progress

4626.fun is pre-launch experimental software. Vaults are not live and there are no live deposits. Audit reports and contract addresses will be published at /security as the protocol approaches mainnet.

Demo vaults are previews

The Akita, Jesse, Gabriel Haines, and Nick Shirley vaults shown across 4626.fun are illustrative previews. None are live. Strategy outcomes shown are illustrative, not historical.
