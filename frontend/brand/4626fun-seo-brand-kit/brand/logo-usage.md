# Logo Usage

## Clear space
Keep at least 10% of the logo width clear on all sides.

## Minimum sizes
- Icon-only mark: 32 px minimum for interface use; 16 px only for favicon use.
- Horizontal lockup: 240 px minimum width.
- Stacked lockup: 180 px minimum width.

## Do not
- Do not stretch or skew the logo.
- Do not place the logo on visually noisy backgrounds without a contrast panel.
- Do not recolor the metallic gold, silver, or blue lines unless producing a monochrome mask.
- Do not use a fake checkerboard preview as a transparency substitute.
