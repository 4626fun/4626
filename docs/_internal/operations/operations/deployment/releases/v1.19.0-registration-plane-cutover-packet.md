Status: **NO-GO — unsigned; no Base mainnet or production-environment mutation authorized**

Prepared: 2026-07-12

## Decision

The registration-plane deployment and wiring are reproducible on a Base mainnet fork, and the
read-only verifier passes all 70 wiring, authorization, codeId, immutable-pointer, adapter, and AKITA
isolation checks. The cutover must not be broadcast yet because the current Phase 1 finalization
cannot complete inside Base's transaction gas ceiling when it deploys the current `CreatorShareOFT`.

The fork transaction reached a 24,489,525 gas limit and reverted with
`Phase1ShareOFTMissing()` (`0x3b818ed0`). Lower gas multipliers failed at 8,386,330,
12,895,224, and 19,336,503 gas with the same error. The error is emitted after the inner ShareOFT
CREATE2 deployment is caught without deployed code. The unrestricted Forge simulation succeeds,
which confirms this is a transaction-gas execution blocker rather than bad constructor arguments or
missing bytecode.

Do not deploy until Phase 1 ShareOFT finalization is split or otherwise proven below Base's live
transaction gas limit.

## Abort and rollback

Before adapter or environment mutation, abort by not broadcasting; the live plane remains unchanged.

After contracts are deployed but before traffic cutover:

1. Do not retarget the adapter.
2. Do not update Vercel/KPR variables.
3. Revoke new factory/batcher authorization if it was granted on a shared mutable contract.
4. Leave deterministic deployments unused; they are not proxies and cannot overwrite live code.

After adapter retarget but before production traffic, restore its prior registry and LM in the same
owner-controlled maintenance window, then rerun the old-plane verifier. Do not attempt to migrate
AKITA.

Once a new vault is created on the new immutable plane, rollback is not a pointer flip. Stop new
deploys, preserve that vault's registry/LM identity, and issue a dedicated remediation plan.
