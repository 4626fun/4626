4626.fun — Brand Inspiration Deck

Prepared: May 6, 2026
For: 4626.fun — a Base-native DeFi protocol built on ERC-4626 tokenized vaults
Goal: Premium and technically credible, but memorable and slightly weird. Logo = a single "4" mark.



TL;DR — Recommended direction



Position 4626.fun as the credible, opinionated home for ERC-4626 vaults on Base — built like infrastructure, branded like a product.

Visually: full-bleed near-black canvas in the LayerZero / Pendle register, broken by one "4" mark in Base Blue — no gradients, no 3D, no rocket ships. Treat the "4" the way Base treats its filled blue Square: a single primitive that scales from 16 px favicon to billboard.

Copy-wise: short imperative fragments in the Base voice (Deposit. Compound. Withdraw.), a hard-numbers trust block in the LayerZero style ($X TVL · Y vaults · Z curators), and one piece of weird microcopy per page so the .fun in the domain isn't a lie.

Avoid: cubes, coins, chains, rockets, neon cyberpunk, 3D glassmorphic vaults, "crypto cute" mascots, Coinbase-as-credibility leaning, or any logo direction that reads as a typed numeral. The "4" must look like a symbol someone could draw from memory after seeing it once.

1. Base — base.org

Base homepage
Base brand guidelines

Hero: "A global economy, built by all of us" / "An open stack for the global economy"
CTAs: "Get Base App" · "Build on Base" (ghost / outlined buttons, no fill)
Nav: Chain · Products · Developers · Solutions · Community · About

Voice — punchy populist manifesto. Short sentences, second-person, zero jargon. Coined vocabulary like "onchain", "open canvas", "open stack". Imperative fragments: Post. Own. Earn. Brand guidelines explicitly demand "progressive, conversational, witty, championing of builders."

Visual. Color is the discipline: Base Blue #0000FF + grayscale, with a secondary palette (red #FC401F, yellow #FFD12F, green #66C800, pink #FEA8CD) used sparingly. The brand rule is literally "think rhythm, not rainbow: lead with monochrome and spotlight one accent." Typography is custom Base Sans (geometric grotesque, Modern Gothic derivation) + Base Mono for code/metadata. The logo is a single filled blue square — simplest possible primitive, strict rules around it.

Trust signaling. Very little hard data on the homepage; trust comes from openness (public brand kit, public source) and ecosystem breadth (App / Build / Chain / Pay).

Steal for 4626.fun





The single-character logo logic. The Square works at 16 px because it has zero internal complexity. The "4" mark must follow the same rule: solid, primitive, no decoration.



Monochrome + one electric accent. Inherit Base Blue or build an adjacent palette; keep secondary colors out of the hero.



Punchy verb-only fragments: Deposit. Compound. Withdraw.

Avoid





The "everything app" vibe — Base spreads across App/Build/Chain/Pay. 4626.fun has one job: vaults.



Leaning on "built on Base" as the value prop. Lead with what the protocol does.

Phase 4 — Brand recommendation for 4626.fun

Positioning statement



4626.fun is the credible home for ERC-4626 vaults on Base.
One standard. Many strategies. Onchain, noncustodial, by default.

This positions the brand on the standards layer (ERC-4626) — not on a specific yield strategy, not on a chain, not on a target user. The standard is the moat. .fun signals you take the standard seriously without taking yourself too seriously.

Vocabulary: vault, deposit, compound, withdraw, noncustodial, composable, onchain, ERC-4626, curator, strategy, share, asset. Borrow onchain from Base. Avoid crypto, web3, DeFi 2.0, revolutionary, next-gen.



Sentence shape: Short. Imperative. Verb-first when possible. One slightly weird microcopy line per page.



Personality budget: 90% serious, 10% strange. The strange 10% is what makes the .fun honest.

Recommended primary mark — The Notched 4 (Direction 1)

Why it wins:





Same logic as Base's Square — a single primitive shape with one rule. It compounds Base's brand language while being unmistakably its own.



The notch carries the meaning. A square with a corner removed is recognizably a 4 because the missing quadrant is the 4's open corner. This is the kind of symbol that works because it's almost not there.



Modular reuse. The notch can become a brand motif — section dividers, card-corners, animation transitions all derived from the same 90° angle.



Favicon dominance. At 16 px it's a solid blue square with a clean nick — no font hinting, no antialiasing problems.



One-color discipline: works as black, white, or Base Blue with zero modification.

Logo system rules (adopted from Base)





Only solid fills — no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects, ever.



Three approved colors: Base Blue (#0000FF), Black (#0A0A0A), White (#FFFFFF). Never grey, never tinted.



The "4" must hold a minimum clear space equal to one of its inner stroke widths.



Never place the blue "4" on a colored background that isn't black or white.



Never typeset "4626.fun" with the mark replacing the first character — the mark is independent of the wordmark.

No 3D, no shadow, no texture.

3. Bracket 4 (corner-mark)



Logo mark: a stylized "4" formed by two right-angle corner brackets — like crop marks or registration marks — arranged so the negative space between them reads unmistakably as the digit 4. Solid color, flat vector, hairline-precise geometry. Architectural blueprint meets Bauhaus. Single color (black or Base blue #0000FF) on white. No gradient, no 3D, no glow.

4. Roman IV inside 4



Logo mark: a geometric digit "4" where the diagonal stem and the vertical line are visually separated as a clean "I" + "V" (Roman numeral four) hidden inside the Arabic 4 silhouette. Subtle — the IV is only visible on a second look. Flat vector, single solid color, no gradient, no shadow. Modernist, witty, Swiss-grid precise. White background.

5. Block 4 (four modules)



Logo mark: a chunky geometric digit "4" constructed from exactly four solid square modules — one per stroke segment — arranged so the four squares spell out the number 4. Modular, voxel-like but flat (not 3D). Solid blue (#0000FF) on white, or black on white. No gradient, no shadow, no isometric perspective. The "four squares making a four" double-meaning is the concept.

6. Cutout 4 (app-icon style)



Logo mark: a solid blue (#0000FF) filled circle or rounded-square app-icon tile with a clean geometric digit "4" knocked out of the center as negative space (white shows through). The 4 is custom-drawn — open-top, geometric, all strokes equal weight, NOT a typed font character. Flat vector, no gradient, no glow, no 3D. Iconic, favicon-ready, app-store-ready.

7. Geometric 4 (purist)



Logo mark: a custom-drawn geometric digit "4" — open top, flat-topped style — where every stroke is the exact same weight and every angle is precisely 90° or 45°. Like a Massimo Vignelli or Wim Crouwel reduction of the number 4. Solid black or solid blue. Flat vector, no gradient, no shadow. Centered on white. Should look hand-drafted from compass and ruler, not typeset.

8. Stencil 4 (the weird option)



Logo mark: a geometric digit "4" with a single intentional 1-unit gap/break in one of its strokes — like a stencil cut. The gap is the only personality. Otherwise extremely clean, geometric, single-color flat vector. Solid black on white. The gap should feel deliberate, not broken. Bauhaus minimalism with one quiet detail.

Iteration tips





Generate 4–8 variants per prompt at low cost first, pick the 2 strongest, then re-prompt with --more variations.



After picking a direction, redraw it manually in Figma — AI marks are starting points, not finals. The final must be a real vector, not a rasterized AI image.



Test every candidate at 16 × 16 px (favicon), 64 × 64 px (Twitter avatar), 512 × 512 px (app icon), and at 200 px tall on a billboard mock. If any size loses its identity, kill it.



Always test the mark in three colorways: black on white, white on black, Base Blue on white.



Phase 7 — Do this / avoid this (cheat sheet)

DO





Treat the "4" the way Base treats its Square — one primitive, three approved colors, strict rules, no decoration.

Show live TVL above the hero headline. Honest, even when small.

Embed a live vault preview in the hero. Aerodrome pattern.

Allow exactly one weird / dry microcopy line per page. That's where the .fun lives.

❌ Aviation / aerospace metaphors (Aerodrome).

❌ Coinbase-as-credibility leaning. Lead with what the protocol does.



❌ All-B2B copy with no retail entry point. The .fun TLD made you a promise.



❌ Marketing site that visually contradicts the app. One brand end-to-end.



❌ Gradients on the logo. Ever.



❌ Pure black (#000000) — use near-black #0A0A0A or #08080B.
