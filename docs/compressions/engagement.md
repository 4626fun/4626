# Engagement Compression

4626 compresses engagement by turning market activity into an onchain game loop.

## Trading Fee Lottery

4626 describes a 6.9% fee on qualifying ShareOFT **buys** (`SwapOnly → non-SwapOnly`) that funds a VRF-backed lottery. Each qualifying buy has an instant win chance; winners are paid in **ShareOFT ■**.

## Why This Matters

- engagement becomes provable and auditable onchain
- incentives are funded by the market, not by offchain promises
