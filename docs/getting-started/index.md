# Getting started

4626 is **creator vault infrastructure on Base**: wrap your Zora creator coin in an ERC-4626 vault, launch shares through a CCA auction, and route trading fees and external revenue to holders.

## What you get

- **Vault shares** (`▢TICKER`) — ERC-4626 claims on vault TVL
- **Share OFT** (`■TICKER`) — cross-chain tradable share (including Solana mesh)
- **Gauge + lottery** — fee routing and holder incentives
- **Strategy sleeves** — Charm + Ajna (paid greenfield bundle)

Read [How it works](/overview/how-it-works) for the full picture.

## Deploy in the app

1. Open **[app.4626.fun/deploy/vault](https://app.4626.fun/deploy/vault)**
2. Sign in and complete wallet signing when prompted
3. Activate **`vault_full_deploy`** at [strategy features](https://app.4626.fun/creator/strategy/features) ($499 USDC bundle)
4. Hold **50M–100M** creator coin on your canonical smart wallet
5. Run deploy — current greenfield batcher is **[v1.14.1](/reference/addresses)**

Then follow [Launch token](/guides/launch-token) and [Activate vault](/guides/activate-vault).
