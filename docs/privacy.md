# Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 4626 ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use 4626 and 4626.fun (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- **Account information**: email address and sign-in metadata.
- **Wallet information**: public wallet addresses and onchain activity (public by nature).
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, features used, and interaction data.
- **Device and network data**: browser type, device identifiers, and IP address (for security and fraud prevention).

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and improve the Service.
- Authenticate users and secure accounts.
- Communicate about updates, security, and support.
- Analyze usage and performance.

## 3. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

- **Service providers** that help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, support).
- **Blockchain networks** where transactions are publicly recorded.
- **Legal and compliance** when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- **Business transfers** in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us. You can also opt out of non-essential communications.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to: 4626dotfun@gmail.com
