Using `swapRouter` as the external-venue `spender` self-revokes the permanent `setSwapPath` allowance — a keeper can persistently brick the primary conversion venue** `CreatorPayoutRouter._convertViaExternalAndQueue` (L514-519) interacting with `setSwapPath` (L257) · Confidence: 75 · **Severity:** Medium · **\[phase2: 2 agents, numerical-gap + economic-security, independently traced identical mechanism\]** **Description** `_convertViaExternalAndQueue`'s per-call approval teardown (`forceApprove(spender, 0)` → `forceApprove(spender, amountIn)` → … → `forceApprove(spender, 0)`, L514/515/519) and `setSwapPath`'s permanent grant (L257) both write to the **same** `(token, spender)` allowance slot whenever `spender == swapRouter`. `forceApprove` assigns absolutely rather than incrementing, so a single external-venue call with `spender = swapRouter` leaves that token's allowance to `swapRouter` at exactly 0 — permanently, since no function ever re-approves it except a fresh `setSwapPath` call (owner-only). **Proof of Concept** `setSwapPath(USDC, path)` sets `USDC.allowance(router, swapRouter) = type(uint256).max` (L257). Keeper calls `convertViaExternalAndQueue({tokenIn: USDC, amountIn: 1, spender: swapRouter, swapTarget: swapRouter, …})`: L514 sets allowance to 0, L515 to 1, L518 the call executes, L519 sets it back to 0\. Post-state: `USDC.allowance(router, swapRouter) == 0`. The next `convertAndQueue(USDC, …)` passes every in-contract check (L456-474) and then reverts inside `swapRouter.exactInput`'s internal `transferFrom` for lack of allowance. Recovery requires an owner `setSwapPath` transaction; a malicious keeper can immediately re-brick it for the cost of 1 wei of `tokenIn` and gas — a persistent, cheaply-repeatable DoS on the primary (uncapped, per Finding 1) conversion venue for that token. **Fix** Have `_convertAndQueue` manage its own per-call approval (`forceApprove(swapRouter, amountIn)` before `exactInput`, `forceApprove(swapRouter, 0)` after) instead of depending on a standing grant from `setSwapPath` — this single change also closes Finding 2's enabling precondition. --- \[70\] **5\. Keeper spend-cap window permits a 2× burst at every boundary; cap reconfiguration silently refunds the keeper's spent budget** `CreatorPayoutRouter._consumeKeeperExternalSpend` (L546-558) / `setKeeperExternalSpendCap` (L296-303) · Confidence: 70 · **Severity:** Low · **\[both — corroborated by 7+ agents across both phases with matching arithmetic\]** **Description** Two related mechanisms in the same accounting path: * **Fixed (non-sliding) window.** `windowStart`/`spentInWindow` reset to `block.timestamp`/`0` the moment `block.timestamp >= windowStart + window` (L547-551). A keeper can spend the full `cap` at `windowEnd - 1` and the full `cap` again one block later at `windowEnd` — **2× the configured cap within seconds.** * **Reconfiguration resets accrued spend.** `setKeeperExternalSpendCap` unconditionally writes `spendCap.windowStart = uint64(block.timestamp); spendCap.spentInWindow = 0;` (L299-300) on **every** call, including a cap _reduction_. An owner responding to suspicious activity by lowering the cap immediately hands the keeper a fresh full window instead of restricting them. **Proof of Concept** `setKeeperExternalSpendCap(USDC, 1000e6, 86400)` at `t=0` → `windowStart=0`. At `t=86399`: `86399 >= 86400` false, keeper spends `1000e6` (cap reached). At `t=86400` (next block, \~2s later on Base): `86400 >= 86400` true → reset → keeper spends `1000e6` again. **2,000e6 USDC moved in \~2 seconds against a cap documented as 1,000e6/day.** Separately: owner sees the burst and calls `setKeeperExternalSpendCap(USDC, 500e6, 86400)` intending to tighten — L299-300 zeroes `spentInWindow`, handing the keeper a third full tranche of 500e6 immediately. **Fix** Use a sliding/decaying window (e.g. `spentInWindow -= min(spentInWindow, cap * elapsed / window)` before adding new spend) instead of a hard reset; in `setKeeperExternalSpendCap`, only initialize `windowStart`/`spentInWindow` when `windowStart == 0`, preserving accrued spend across cap adjustments. --- \[55\] **6\. `CreatorCoinPolicyController`'s revenue-redirect handoff carries no timelock — a compromised controller-owner key redirects the entire future revenue stream instantly, with no delay comparable to the router's 1-day emergency-withdraw window** `CreatorCoinPolicyController.acceptCreatorCoinOwnership` (L67-74) / `proposeCreatorCoinOwnershipTransfer` (L60-64) · Confidence: 55 · **Severity: Low (centralization/control-design observation — requires the trusted controller-owner key, not an unprivileged exploit path)** **Description** The controller's own `Ownable` owner can call `proposeCreatorCoinOwnershipTransfer(self)` then `acceptCreatorCoinOwnership()` (gated only by `msg.sender == pendingCreatorCoinOwner`, L69) in the same or next transaction — no delay, no minimum notice — becoming the CreatorCoin's direct owner and then calling `creatorCoin.setPayoutRecipient(attacker)` itself, permanently diverting **all future external-earnings revenue** away from the router/burn-stream lane.
