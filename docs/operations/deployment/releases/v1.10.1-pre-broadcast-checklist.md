## 3. Skip Vanity Decision

- [ ] Broadcast uses generic CREATE2 salts via:


Decision D2b: v1.10.1 deliberately skips the salt grinder and accepts generic addresses to compress the broadcast window. See the v1.10.1 runbook decision stack in `docs/operations/deployment/v1.10.1/deployment-instructions.md`.
